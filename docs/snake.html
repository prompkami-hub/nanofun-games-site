THE LITTLE ARCADE / GAME GUIDE

Snake

Pick a direction, collect the next fruit, and leave room for the return journey. The snake grows as you eat, so a simple route becomes a moving puzzle. Your score belongs to the run you actually play.

Open the arcade & choose Snake →

How to play

  1. Guide the snake to fruit to grow and score.
  2. Hitting a wall or the snake’s own body ends the run. Watch the space ahead of the head, not only the next fruit.
  3. As the run progresses, movement gets faster. Choose turns early and leave yourself a way out.

Keyboard & phone controls

Swipe, use the on-screen directional buttons, or press the arrow keys or WASD. Pause and Resume keep the same run; Restart begins a new one.

Try these small improvements

Keep the center open

Long early loops around the board can preserve space. Avoid dividing a large open area into two narrow pockets.

Follow the moving tail

A space occupied now may open as the tail advances. Plan for movement, while allowing extra room when eating makes the snake grow.

Skip the risky shortcut

The shortest line to a fruit can trap a long snake. A wider turn is often easier to control as the pace increases.

Free coins & starting a game

One coin starts a run. Pause and Resume are free. Starting again after a collision spends one coin. If the cabinet does not load

Check that JavaScript is enabled and your connection is available, then return to the arcade. If a round is waiting for confirmation, retry that same round instead of repeatedly starting a new one. Do not pay again just because a game or order is slow.
