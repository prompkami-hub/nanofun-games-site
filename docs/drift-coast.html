Drift Coast.

A coastal road, a tight corner, and one well-timed boost. Drive a lightweight arcade racer against five computer rivals. Choose Sunset Marina for warm coastlines or Alpine Switchback for a higher mountain circuit, then race one lap or three.

Choose Drift Coast in the arcade

Keyboard controls

  • Arrows or WASD: accelerate, brake and steer.
  • Shift plus steering: drift. Drift through a corner to build nitro charge.
  • Space: spend a nitro bottle for a burst of speed. You can carry two bottles.
  • Release a drift held for more than half a second for a short mini turbo.
  • R: bring your car back to the road center without starting a new race.
  • Escape: pause or resume. Switching tabs also pauses.

On a phone

The throttle is automatic. Hold the left steering area and slide your finger left or right; release to center the steering. Use Drift while steering, and tap the Nitro button for a boost. Separate fingers can steer and drift together. Both portrait and landscape are supported; landscape gives a wider view of the next corner.

Find your racing line

Watch the road ahead and the minimap. Wide exits and collisions slow you down. Use short steering corrections, save nitro for a straighter section, and use a drift release to carry speed out of a corner. The time, position and lap counter show this race only. Results and best laps are saved locally in your browser, not submitted to an online leaderboard.

Coins, restarts & compatibility

One coin starts the race with your selected lap count. Pause and resume are free. Starting another race uses a new coin, so return to the menu only when you intend to end the current run. Guest wallets start with five free coins; verified accounts can claim ten daily. Coins have no cash value.

Drift Coast requires WebGL and a recent browser. If 3D graphics are unavailable, choose 2048 or Snake. No advertising SDK is loaded by this game.

About this game

Drift Coast is an original NanoFun arcade racer. Its cars, tracks and scenery are generated in code, and its engine tones and electronic music are synthesized in the browser. It does not use QQ Speed names, maps, models, music or source code and is not affiliated with that game. The 3D renderer uses Three.js under the MIT license.
