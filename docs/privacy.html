This notice describes how NanoFun Games handles information for this website and its wallet. Questions or requests: superpowergames.us@gmail.com.

What we use

  • Guest identity: a random browser identifier is used to keep a guest balance. A derived identifier, rather than that browser identifier itself, is stored with the wallet.
  • Email account: your email, verified account identifier, coin balance, VIP expiry and account activity are used for sign-in, restoring access and customer support.
  • Billing: relevant Stripe order, customer, subscription, paid-period and refund/dispute references are used to verify purchases. Stripe handles payment details; NanoFun does not collect or store your full card number or CVC.
  • Mail: we send requested sign-in codes and transactional purchase confirmations. A purchase does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Browser storage

This site stores a signed sign-in session, a guest device identifier, language and game preferences, local scores, and pending game-start identifiers in your browser. These are used to restore access, retain preferences and avoid duplicate coin charges on a retry. Sign out to remove the local account session. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and account sessions expire after seven days.

Clearing browser storage may lose guest access, scores and preferences. An email account can sign in again to restore its server-recorded balance and purchases.

Service providers

Google services process wallet records and transactional email. Stripe processes payments and the order details needed for verification. GitHub Pages hosts the public website. These providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services under their own privacy policies.

Access and retention

Account and transaction records are used for wallet operation, support, reconciliation and dispute handling. Player records are private and are not displayed in public account lists. We retain records while needed for those purposes and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact us using the account email. We may ask you to verify that you own the account. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting or dispute obligations. Do not include payment-card details or sign-in codes in your request.

Children

If a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, a parent or guardian can contact us to request review and removal where applicable. Purchases should be made by an adult authorized to use the payment method.
