THE LITTLE ARCADE / GAME GUIDE

2048

A small board, one swipe, and a decision that changes the next move. Slide matching numbered tiles together and build toward 2048. There is no timer; the challenge is keeping enough space for the next tile.

Open the arcade & choose 2048 →

How to play

  1. Move all tiles in one direction. Equal adjacent tiles combine once during a move.
  2. A valid move brings a new tile onto the board. Plan the whole row or column, rather than following just one pair.
  3. A run ends when the board is full and no neighboring tiles can merge. Reaching 2048 lets you continue the same run.

Keyboard & phone controls

On a phone, swipe the board. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD. Pause before leaving the screen; choose Resume to continue the same run.

Try these small improvements

Keep one strong corner

Try keeping your largest tile in one corner. Keep the neighboring row in descending order so smaller tiles can feed into it.

Leave a route back

Before a swipe, check whether it will separate a useful pair or move the largest tile away from its corner.

Space beats a quick merge

A small merge is not always the best move. An empty row or an accessible pair can keep the next turn possible.

Free coins & starting a game

One coin starts a run. Reaching 2048 and continuing that run does not spend another coin. A restart is a new run. New guest wallets receive 5 welcome coins; verified email accounts can claim 10 free coins once per Los Angeles day. You can read this guide without an account, cookies or JavaScript. If the cabinet does not load

Check that JavaScript is enabled and your connection is available, then return to the arcade. If a round is waiting for confirmation, retry that same round instead of repeatedly starting a new one. Do not pay again just because a game or order is slow.
